# [[Projects/Languages/Slovura/Slovura Lessons/Slovura 12 - Singular and Plural\|Slovura 12 - Singular and Plural]]

[[Projects/Languages/Slovura/Slovura\|Slovura]] does not require number to be specified.

To explicitly express multiple members of a concept, add `-te`.

> **-[[Projects/Languages/Slovura/Slovura Roots/te\|te]]** — plural

[[Projects/Languages/Slovura/Slovura Roots/te\|te]] is a [[Projects/Languages/Slovura/Slovura Lessons/Slovura 10 - Whole-Word Modifiers\|whole-word modifier]].

## Making a Word Plural

> **[[Projects/Languages/Slovura/Slovura Roots/ka\|ka]]**
>
> person

> **kate**
>
> `ka` + `te`
>
> **people / persons**

Without `-te`, plurality is not explicitly marked.

## Plural Compound Words

The same rule applies to compounds:

> **[[Projects/Languages/Slovura/Slovura Words/kasapa\|kasapa]]**
>
> sibling

> **kasapate**
>
> `(kasapa) te`
>
> **siblings**

The concept is constructed first and then pluralized as a whole.

## Stacking with Gender

Whole-word modifiers can stack:

> **kasapano** — brother
>
> **kasapanote** — `((kasapa) no) te` — **brothers**

> **kasapana** — sister
>
> **kasapanate** — `((kasapa) na) te` — **sisters**

The structure is:

> **word + [[Projects/Languages/Slovura/Slovura Lessons/Slovura 11 - Gender\|gender]] + plural**

## Remember

> [!summary]
> - `-te` explicitly marks plurality.
> - An unmarked word does not explicitly specify number.
> - `ka` → person; `kate` → people.
> - `kasapa` → sibling; `kasapate` → siblings.
> - Whole-word modifiers can stack: `kasapano` → `kasapanote`.
